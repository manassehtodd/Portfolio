Good questions

Do I need to book?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booked clients go first. Booking takes about 20 seconds on WhatsApp.

Can I play without a haircut?

Yes. The lounge is open to everyone during shop hours, and you pay by the half hour.

How do crypto payments work?

Ask for the wallet address at the counter. Send the Naira price in USDT (TRC-20) or BTC at the day's rate, and show the confirmation before you leave.

Do you do home service?

Yes, by arrangement. Send a WhatsApp message with your location and the time you want.
