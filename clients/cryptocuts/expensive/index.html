        06 — FAQ

        Asked & answered

        Do I need to book, or can I walk in?

        Walk-ins are welcome, but booked clients go first. Booking takes three taps and a WhatsApp message.

        Can I use the lounge without a haircut?

        Yes. The PS5s are open to everyone during shop hours, and you pay by the half hour.

        How does crypto payment work?

        Ask for the wallet address at the counter. Send the Naira price in USDT (TRC-20) or BTC at the day's rate, then show the confirmation. Cash, transfer and POS are always fine too.

        Do you do home service?

        Yes, by arrangement. Send your location and preferred time on WhatsApp.
